AnimeAn, in its own window

A replica of the AnimeAn interface, played by one timeline: the interface and free layout, the painting tools, three ways to Auto Map a pattern, folds, and the Free Ground Line. H/V axes and Additional Lines use the “A” from the sample project; contours map a teapot onto a flag that waves frame by frame. Pick a chapter, or click inside one to jump.
